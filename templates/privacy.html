Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1Data We Collect

When you register, we collect your name and email address. As you use Spendly, we store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not collect payment card details or access your bank accounts.

2How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only to run Spendly: to authenticate you, to show your expenses and spending summaries, and to improve the reliability of the service. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

3Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application's database, and passwords are stored in hashed form rather than plain text. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You can delete your expenses at any time.

4Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when a page loads. We do not share your expense data with any other third party, except where required by law.

5Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want your data removed, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
